Main improvements:
1. Tighten converter and eFuse placement/routing
Complete gate paths 24.148 / 28.335 mm; gate-resistor-to-FET paths 19.610 / 26.329 mm. eFuse output bypass designated trace path 23.026 mm, two transfers. VC compensation route 15.185 mm.
Move bypass/timing/compensation components close to their pins and shorten gate/return loops. Any replacement needs a new clearance and reference-path check.

2. Reduce routing through the main ground plane
327.930 mm of non-ground signals on inner1; two TEC_SAFE_EN trunks total 124.012 mm.
Relocate avoidable signal corridors off the principal reference plane and verify return continuity.

3. Reduce avoidable detours and transfers
Fan feed 90.592 mm vs 36.989 mm endpoint distance; UART_TX 56.079 mm vs 31.546 mm.
Explore shorter legal routes while preserving intentional analog/switching isolation. Geometric chord is not proof a direct legal route exists.

4. Increase tight clearance and debug-probe margins where space permits
Closest foreign via copper gap 0.154204 mm; some pad escapes at 0.200 mm. ADC/I2C probes lack close ground partners in several locations.
Increase available spacing, improve probe-ground access and simplify redundant same-rail branches.
