USB-C PD Peltier driver · probes and fan

Actual PCB placement with one TEC1-12706, two wired DS18B20 probes, a Noctua NF-A8 PWM fan and separate USB-C cables for PD power and program/debug.

USB connections: black cable: PD power from a 100 W charger. Light gray cable: program/debug to a PC. Both use the Cable Matters 201440, specified for 5 A power and USB 2.0 data. Role colors, plugs and short cable segments are illustrations.

HOT/COLD probes: two HiLetgo DS18B20 probes with their 1 m factory cables. At each sensor terminal, pin 1 is red / 3.3 V, pin 2 yellow / DATA, and pin 3 black / GND. The selected listing does not verify housing dimensions: the shown Ø6 × 50 mm cylinders are illustrative. Cable diameters, shortened routes and probe positions also illustrate connections; establish thermal contact with the corresponding hot and cold surfaces in the physical assembly.

Fan: one Noctua NF-A8 PWM 12 V, connected to FAN with its factory four-pin cable. The fixed 12 V output has a 0.5 A continuous budget; this fan is rated at 0.08 A maximum. Black is ground, yellow +12 V, green tach and blue PWM. Tach/PWM are unconnected on the board, so the fan runs at full speed while qualified PD is present. The fan uses manufacturer CAD; its cable route, plug detail and exploded position illustrate connectivity. The factory cable is 200 mm, with a separate 300 mm extension supplied in the box.

Model provenance: the PCB comes directly from the circuit build, using supplier meshes where available. Copper test pads are part of its surface texture and have no artificial bodies. The HiLetgo TEC1-12706 is a 40 × 40 × 3.6 mm listing envelope. Probe and cable shapes are illustrative; the manufacturer fan model preserves external dimensions with simplified internal details. This visualization is not mechanical fit qualification.

Red TEC lead: positive. Black TEC lead: negative. Cable slack, charger and PC ends are omitted. A suitable hot-side heatsink, clamping and thermal interface are required before operation; they are not specified by this visualization.
