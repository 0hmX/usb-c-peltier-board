# TEC1-12706 USB-C PD power board

## Product scope

One inexpensive, commonly sold module: **TEC1-12706, 40 × 40 mm, 12 V / 6 A class**. The value is replacing its dedicated 12 V supply with a USB-C PD charger or power bank. This revision supplies power without temperature regulation, a software interface or polarity reversal.

[ProtoSupplies sells the TEC1-12706](https://protosupplies.com/product/tec1-12706-thermoelectric-peltier-cooling-device/) and calls it the most commonly used TEC module. Availability from multiple hobby suppliers supports this selection; there is no audited global sales ranking. Obtain samples from that one supplier for qualification. Sharing the TEC1-12706 marking does not guarantee identical specifications across manufacturers.

The [Hebei manufacturer reference](https://www.hebeiltd.com.cn/peltier.datasheet/TEC1-12706.pdf) lists 6.4 A maximum, 14.4 V maximum and 50 W maximum heat pumping at a 25 °C hot side. These are specified test conditions, not a promise of 50 W cooling in an arbitrary assembly. Check the 12 V operating point against the actual purchased module and measured current.

## Power path

`100 W PD source → 5 A USB-C cable → CYPD3177-controlled PFET pair → 12 V buck → TEC1-12706`

- CYPD3177 pins 1/2 (VBUS_MIN/MAX) and pin 5 (ISNK_COARSE) tie to VDDD; pin 6 (ISNK_FINE) ties to GND. Datasheet tables 2–4 select exactly 20 V and request 5 A, without resistor ladders or a host processor.
- Back-to-back **P-channel** AO4407A FETs share sources and gates. Separate drains connect raw and qualified VBUS. A 49.9 kΩ gate-to-source resistor keeps them off without gate drive. VDC_OUT senses the qualified rail through 1 kΩ.
- SAFE_PWR_EN fallback power is absent. Specify CYPD3177 firmware 3.10D or later/date code after 2047; [Infineon's firmware errata](https://community.infineon.com/t5/Knowledge-Base-Articles/CYPD3177-EZ-PD-BCR-Device-Firmware-Updates/ta-p/251759) explain older minimum-voltage behavior.
- FAULT is active high when requested voltage/current is unavailable and drives an NPN clamp on buck EN. A 130 kΩ / 10 kΩ EN divider independently rejects startup 5 V and low-voltage sources. Characterize thresholds including EN bias and tolerances. Source loss and protocol resets must remove load power.
- TPS56837 uses TI's 12 V / 500 kHz LC/feedback combination: 5.6 µH, two 22 µF output capacitors, 383 kΩ / 20 kΩ feedback and 30 pF feedforward. Nominal output is **12.09 V**. The 10 kΩ MODE setting selects the lower internal current-limit option; 100 nF SS gives about 10 ms soft start.
- Buck overcurrent/hiccup and thermal shutdown protect the converter. Its valley-current limit is tolerance dependent and is **not** a precise 6 A load limit. Qualify module current across temperature. Add a dedicated load-current limit if module/cable tests require it.

12 V × 6 A = 72 W electrical output. At an assumed 90% converter efficiency that requires 80 W before PFET/connector losses. Requesting 20 V / 5 A gives headroom. Measure efficiency; 90% is a budgeting assumption. A 65 W source or 3 A cable is insufficient for this target and must not enable the load.

Use the purchased TEC's supplied leads directly; the seller does not specify a gauge or length that can be assumed across batches. Extensions require separately qualified cable/current/temperature ratings. Do not use Qwiic/JST-SH signal cables for this load. Heatsink, clamp, interface material and airflow belong to the user's thermal installation. This board has no fan connector or fan control.

USB, PD, buck, FET and output capacitor definitions use imported supplier footprints under `imports/`. Connector/FET element types and pin roles are adapted without changing imported copper. The inductor uses a manufacturer-derived land pattern because the catalog part has no importable EasyEDA component: two 3.55 × 3.5 mm lands, a 5.4 mm gap and 12.5 mm overall span. Its 3D body is a datasheet envelope, not detailed supplier CAD. Generic small passives still need purchasing numbers and DC-bias/tolerance verification. The parts engine warns about several JLCPCB availability records and the absent inductor supplier footprint; catalog sourcing and the manufacturer-derived footprint must be qualified separately.

## Qualification before manufacture

Complete copper routing, return paths, feedback placement, transient/ESD review and thermal spreading. Test output regulation/ripple and module current across temperature; startup/detach/weak-source rejection; both USB orientations; connector and lead heating; short circuits; sustained load at intended ambient temperature; capacitor effective values; and PFET gate transients.

The board does not detect a missing heatsink or stop at a target temperature. The thermal installation must provide suitable operation and protection. Four-layer 60 × 40 mm is a prototype placement choice. Source/netlist and placement checks do not constitute analogue simulation, USB certification or bench qualification.
